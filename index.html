About Caspian Water Recovery

×

Geospatial Decision Support System

This application was developed for international environmental technology hackathons to demonstrate the potential for atmospheric water recovery from the Caspian Sea.

Key Features:

  • Interactive geospatial monitoring of evaporation patterns
  • Hotspot detection and ranking system
  • Condensation potential index calculation
  • AI-assisted decision support engine
  • Technology selection recommendations
  • Economic modeling for pilot projects
  • Real-time weather monitoring

Scientific Approach:

The system identifies areas with high evaporation rates as potential evaluation zones for atmospheric water recovery technologies. It does not equate evaporation directly to recoverable water, but rather uses evaporation as an indicator of atmospheric water availability.

Disclaimer:

This is a prototype demonstration system. All data should be validated through field measurements before any implementation decisions. The economic models and technology recommendations are preliminary assessments requiring detailed engineering studies.
